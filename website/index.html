Before you switch

What about.

The five things people ask before they trust their billing history to a folder on their disk.

What does it cost? +

CHF 50, once. That's it. No monthly bill, no yearly renewal, no "premium plan". Future updates are free for as long as we keep making them.

What if my laptop dies? +

Your invoices sit in a folder on your computer, so you back them up the same way you back up your other important folders. iCloud, Dropbox, Time Machine, a USB drive. Anything that saves a copy of a folder will save your invoices.

What if Fucktura disappears one day? +

Nothing changes. Your invoices are files in your folder, saved in a format any computer can read. You can open them, print them, or move them to another tool. Nothing is locked inside Fucktura.

Does it handle Swiss QR bills? +

Yes. Every Swiss invoice includes the QR payment slip at the bottom. Your clients scan it with their banking app and pay without typing account numbers.

How does the client receive the invoice? +

Fucktura saves it as a PDF. You send the PDF however you already send things: email, WhatsApp, Signal, a client portal, printed and posted. Nothing new to learn.

Is there a catch? +

One small one. Fucktura runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, or Opera. Safari and Firefox don't yet let it save files straight to your computer. If you already use one of the first five, you're good.
